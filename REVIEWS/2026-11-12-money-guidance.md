# Money and paperwork guidance review

Reviewed: 12 November 2026. Reference: the owner-provided DenkVoraus Money Guide for Indian Professionals in Germany, edition 1.0. The new prompts and explanations are original FinBridge text. The third-party guide is an editorial reference; primary sources determine legal claims.

## Changes

- A sixteen-point review saves only status choices and selects three follow-up actions. An answer is not an eligibility decision, tax refund forecast or financial-readiness score.
- The existing payslip article now explains seven tax/social-insurance lines, gross bases, employee versus employer amounts, identifiers and payout adjustments.
- Saved evidence groups supplement second-household and departure journeys. The existing family-support checklist adds a tax-year check and payment reconciliation; its counter updates immediately.
- Arrival/departure tax-year preparation, ordinary German-language learning, statutory pension refunds, investment-fund exit taxation and household health-cover questions receive dated, linked guidance.
- Existing newcomer and healthcare-professional routes are retained. Professional pension arrangements are distinguished from statutory pension rules.

## Primary-source decisions

| Topic | Editorial treatment | Primary source |
|---|---|---|
| Payslip figures | Gross tax/social bases, net pay and payout are distinct; employer payments must not be subtracted twice. | [Entgeltbescheinigungsverordnung section 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/entgbv/__1.html) |
| Care insurance | The childless surcharge is paid by the employee; the ordinary split has a Saxony exception. | [SGB XI section 58](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_11/__58.html) |
| Main home abroad | Apply the household and work-related eligibility tests; the distance worksheet excludes flight kilometres. | [EStG section 9](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__9.html) and [BMF LStH 2026](https://esth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/A-Einkommensteuergesetz/II-Einkommen-2-24b/4-Ueberschuss-d-Einnahmen-ueber-die-Werbungsk-8-9a/Paragraf-9/inhalt.html) |
| German learning | General German-language learning is normally personal expenditure; assess specific professional training separately. | [BMF LStH 2026: Deutschkurs](https://lsth.bundesfinanzministerium.de/lsth/2026/A-Einkommensteuergesetz/II-Einkommen-2-24b/4-Ueberschuss-d-Einnahmen-ueber-die-Werbungsk-8-9a/Paragraf-9/inhalt.html) |
| Part-year tax | Foreign income can affect progression; no guaranteed arrival/departure-year refund. | [EStG section 32b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__32b.html) |
| Voluntary returns | Distinguish the four-year voluntary window from mandatory filing obligations. | [EStG section 46](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__46.html) and [Finanzamt BW: Antragsveranlagung](https://finanzamt-bw.fv-bwl.de/%2CLfr/Was%2Bist%2Beine%2BAntragsveranlagung_) |
| Pension refund | The India agreement does not automatically exclude a refund. Contribution history, residence, compulsory/voluntary insurance and waiting periods matter. | [DRV: Beitragserstattung Indien](https://rvrecht.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/SharedDocs/rvRecht/02_GRA_EU_SVA/04_Laender/Indien/gra_indien_15_byes.html) |
| Professional pension | Statutory-pension exemption depends on the particular covered employment or activity. | [DRV: section 6 SGB VI](https://rvrecht.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/SharedDocs/rvRecht/01_GRA_SGB/06_SGB_VI/pp_0001_25/gra_sgb006_p_0006.html) |
| Investment-fund departure | Do not claim an ordinary ETF portfolio is categorically outside exit tax. Review section 19(3), its fund holding/cost tests and the other applicable requirements. | [InvStG section 19](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/invstg_2018/__19.html) |
| Family health cover | Residence, income and other conditions determine family cover; request individual insurer confirmation. | [Federal family-insurance portal](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/de/leistung/99134042017000) |
