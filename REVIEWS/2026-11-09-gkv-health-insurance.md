# GKV health-insurance comparison — 9 November 2026

## Scope and approval status
Educational and noncommercial comparison of six public insurers: TK, Barmer, DAK-Gesundheit, hkk, HEK and Audi BKK. Only 2026 insurer-specific Zusatzbeitrag rates have been validated; **no individual 2027 insurer rate is claimed confirmed**. Not exhaustive, not a recommendation or a promise of coverage. Product terms and benefit limitations can change.

### Verified rates as of 9 Nov 2026
- TK **2.69%**, Barmer **3.29%**, DAK **3.20%**, hkk **2.59%**, HEK **2.89%**, Audi BKK **2.60%**.
- GKV-Spitzenverband statutory insurer register (dated 2026-11-09): https://www.gkv-spitzenverband.de/service/krankenkassenliste/krankenkassen.jsp?filter=17&pageNo=2
- Official BMG 2026 general rate **14.6%**, average Zusatzbeitrag **2.9%**, annual BBG **€69,750**: https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/beitraege/seite

### 2027 status — distinguish confirmations and proposals
- BMG set **2027 average Zusatzbeitrag at 2.9%**, unchanged from 2026, **not** an insurer-specific rate: https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/presse/pressemitteilungen/zusatzbeitragssatz-gkv-2027-festgelegt (2026-10-30).
- Government's draft/approved-at-cabinet 2027 social-insurance thresholds propose GKV BBG **€76,500/year** vs **€69,750/year** in 2026. **Final approval status must be rechecked:** https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Gesetze-und-Gesetzesvorhaben/sozialversicherungs-rechengroessenverordnung-2027.html.
- Every 2027 insurer comparison is labeled a **scenario**, using either a user-entered additional rate or an explicitly marked carry-forward of the 2026 rate, never labeled confirmed.

## Calculation scope and tests
Ordinary employee in German GKV, half general rate and half Zusatzbeitrag, equal monthly wages, no other contribution categories included.

Employee annual GKV premium = min(annual salary, contribution ceiling) × (14.6% + insurer Zusatzbeitrag) ÷ 2.
- €60,000 2026 TK: €5,187/year.
- €80,000 2026 TK: €6,029.8875/year before payroll rounding.
- €80,000 2027 TK *illustrative unchanged-rate scenario*: €6,613.425/year before rounding, assuming proposed 2027 ceiling takes effect.
- €80,000 2026 Barmer: €6,239.1375/year before rounding.

2027 delta versus 2026 combines rate and ceiling effects, not only switching savings. Note that income-tax impacts, Pflegeversicherung, wage payment timing and rounded payroll numbers are excluded.

## Switching regulations
BMG: ordinary 12-month binding period, statutory switching timetable, special right when the *insurer itself* raises Zusatzbeitrag; new insurer normally handles electronic notice: https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/themen/krankenversicherung/online-ratgeber-krankenversicherung/krankenversicherung/wahl-und-wechsel-der-krankenkasse
