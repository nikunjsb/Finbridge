## Numerical scope and sources

- **German 2026 tariff:** [EStG §32a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__32a.html), annual taxable income with statutory truncation and optional eligible splitting. This is not gross salary, payroll withholding, total assessment or a refund. Ordinary extra employment expenses account for the [€1,230 allowance](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__9a.html); separately deductible union dues need their own verified additional amount.
- **Insurance/pension and childcare:** [EStG §10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__10.html). Insurance/pension reviews return net own spending and review steps, never an automatic extra deduction. Domestic childcare uses 80% of eligible net costs, capped at €4,800 per child across both parents, with household/qualifying-period, invoice and bank-payment screening. Foreign-child and unclear cases receive review rather than a numerical deduction.
- **Ordinary donations:** [EStG §10b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__10b.html), with the 20% Gesamtbetrag-der-Einkünfte branch, refunds, other donations and evidence. Political donations, foundations, excluded memberships and unconfirmed foreign-recipient eligibility are outside the numerical model.
- **Families, studies and part years:** [§26](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__26.html), [§10](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__10.html), [§32b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__32b.html) and [§46](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__46.html). First education, subsequent education and education within employment are kept separate; no guaranteed loss carryforward, filing deadline or refund is inferred.
- **India residence:** [Income Tax Department FAQs](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/all-topics/e-filing-services/non%20resident%20-faq). Years beginning before 1 April 2026 remain under the 1961 Act; the 2026–27 screen names section 6 of the 2025 Act. Unknown history/income/liability questions stop unsupported conclusions. The deemed-resident branch is applied only after ordinary day tests fail.
- **FEMA/accounts/remittances:** [RBI non-resident accounts FAQ](https://www.rbi.org.in/commonman/english/scripts/FAQs.aspx?Id=3). Banking status, income-tax residence, permissible credits and remittance routes are separate. The transfer comparison uses user-entered all-in debit/fees/FX, never live prices or a provider recommendation.
- **International income and investments:** [BMF Germany–India treaty](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Standardartikel/Themen/Steuern/Internationales_Steuerrecht/Staatenbezogene_Informationen/Laender_A_Z/Indien/1996-05-06-Indien-Abkommen-DBA.html), [EStG §20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__20.html) and [InvStG §20](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/invstg_2018/__20.html). These are preparation/literacy paths; no foreign-tax-credit, treaty allocation, fund classification or capital-gains calculation is certified.

Rules checked on 13 November 2026. Historical expense models retain their existing 2024–26 year parameters; new German numerical checks are explicitly 2026-only.

A further synthetic payroll case was independently checked in the [official BMF 2026 payroll calculator](https://www.bmf-steuerrechner.de/bl/bl2026/eingabeformbl2026.xhtml): €4,000 monthly gross, class I, statutory insurance, 2.9% additional health rate, childless care surcharge outside Saxony, no church tax/allowances. BMF returned €524.50 wage tax and €0 solidarity/church tax, matching the existing public salary tool. The tool’s separately calculated social shares lead to €2,605.50 monthly net for this example. This single payroll case does not validate all classes or special pay.

Thus €2,000 qualifying ordinary employment expenses above an already-accounted-for €1,230 allowance add €770 of deduction in this specific illustration, changing tariff tax by €270, not €2,000. Additional bracket boundaries were independently worked from the statute using Python Decimal and stored as fixed test expectations.

The suite also checks donation/childcare caps, missing evidence, reimbursements, unsupported assessments, 120/182-day and prior-year residence cases, CSV/year isolation and escaped previews. Financial examples include one-way commuting (€1,710 in 2026 and €1,470 in 2024/25 for 30 km × 150 days), home-office cap, 2026 social-insurance ceilings/child/Saxony shares, published premium examples, the €7,210 double-household example and independently evaluated annuity values.

Social-rate sources: [BMAS 2026 ceilings](https://www.bmas.de/DE/Service/Gesetze-und-Gesetzesvorhaben/sozialversicherungs-rechengroessenverordnung-2026.html), [BMG care contributions](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/themen/pflege/online-ratgeber-pflege/die-pflegeversicherung/finanzierung), and existing insurer-source references in the 9 November review.
