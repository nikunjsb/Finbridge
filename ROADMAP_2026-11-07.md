## Product direction
**FinBridge: a practical Germany × India expat finance, bureaucracy, and relocation companion.** Make complicated tasks understandable with verified sources, guided checklists, examples, and optional local-first calculations.

## Current baseline — avoid duplicating
FinBridge already has tax expense records and an initial commuting/home-office calculator, Germany newcomer and return-to-India checklists, budget/investment illustrations, Germany–India articles, curated expat updates, privacy/local backup, and a Cloudflare → private GitHub feedback pipeline. Extend these instead of creating redundant pages.

## Priority 1 — highest-value visitor features
- [ ] **P1-01 Germany × India tax-residency decision guide:** Start with annual dates, residence ties and country-specific tests; show Germany and India separately, then treaty tie-breaker educational overview. Clearly distinguish NR / RNOR / ROR and German residence; use correct tax-year definitions and special cases. Avoid presenting a definitive residency/legal opinion.
  - **Done when:** branching guide, worked examples, official citations and a printable summary exist.
- [ ] **P1-02 NRE / NRO / FCNR account explorer:** Explain eligibility, permissible funds, interest taxation, repatriation constraints, return-to-India account changes, and RBI/source date. Include examples for employee, student, returning resident.
  - **Done when:** side-by-side scenarios, official RBI links, and banking-status transition checklist are available.
- [ ] **P1-03 Family support and transfers guide:** Sending money Germany→India, gift/support distinctions, evidence checklist, fees/exchange rate comparison framework, German maintenance deduction eligibility and Indian-side implications. Do not label all transfers tax-deductible.
  - **Done when:** step-by-step evidence checklist and 3 worked examples are verified against official sources.
- [ ] **P1-04 German salary explanation and net-pay estimator:** Gross→net breakdown: Lohnsteuer, social contributions, health-insurance Zusatzbeitrag, tax class, children, church tax, annual changes. Show assumptions and avoid precise-looking unsupported results.
  - **Done when:** 2026 parameters sourced, test cases validated, and breakdown understandable to newcomers.
- [ ] **P1-05 Tax workspace enhancements:** Year-specific rules and guided calculators for professional education, insurance, moving costs, home office, pension-related expenses; receipt checklist/export. Separate money spent, eligible amount, and actual tax saving.
  - **Done when:** each calculator lists assumptions, limitations, current law and source links.
- [ ] **P1-06 Newcomer and returnee checklists v2:** Add optional arrival/move dates, contextual deadlines, exact documents and official links, status/filter by employee/student/family, and print/export; keep user entries local.
  - **Done when:** 5 representative user paths produce relevant tasks without irrelevant ones.
- [ ] **P1-07 Explain German letters and forms:** Bilingual plain-language dictionary for Finanzamt, Bürgeramt, Ausländerbehörde, Krankenkasse, DRV, electricity and landlord letters; sample templates in German/English. Explicitly avoid claiming to read or understand documents without data, and avoid uploading sensitive paperwork.
  - **Done when:** common letters have source-based instructions and downloadable/copyable templates.

## Guardrails
- Educational not individual financial/tax/legal advice; never claim guaranteed deductions or residency status.
- Prefer official government primary sources, state last verification date and applicable year.
- No personal financial information to GitHub Issues; no secret keys in client code.
- No background data harvesting; optional local-first progress and backups.
